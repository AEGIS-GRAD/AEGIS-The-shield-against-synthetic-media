# Questionnaire

All Likert items use a 7-point scale: 1 Strongly disagree · 2 Disagree · 3 Somewhat disagree · 4 Neutral · 5 Somewhat agree · 6 Agree · 7 Strongly agree.

## A. Background (before the cases)

**How much experience do you have checking whether images, audio or video are genuine?**
None / A little (curious or occasional) / Some (regular part of my studies or work) / A lot (it is my job or main focus)

**Have you used an automated deepfake or AI-content detector before?**
Never / Once or twice / Several times / Regularly

## B. After every case (8 times)

**Based on this result, what would you do with this media?**
- Treat it as authentic
- Treat it as manipulated
- I can't decide without further checks

Rate (1–7):
- I trust this result.
- I understand why the system gave this result.
- I am confident in the decision I just made.

## C. Final questionnaire (once)

Rated twice, once for each format. Block order alternates between participants (see README).

**The full evidence report** and **The single score**, each:
1. I could rely on {the full evidence report | the single score} when deciding what to do with a piece of media.
2. {The full evidence report | The single score} helped me understand why the system reached its result.
3. {The full evidence report | The single score} made it clear when I should be careful.
4. {The full evidence report | The single score} was easy to read.
5. {The full evidence report | The single score} gave me about the right amount of information.
6. When the system was unsure or its signals conflicted, {the full evidence report | the single score} helped me decide what to do next.
7. I would be comfortable using {the full evidence report | the single score} in my own work.

## D. Forced choice

**If you could have only one of these for real work, which would you choose?**
- The full evidence report
- The single score

In a sentence or two, why?

Was anything in this study confusing or hard to understand? (optional)
